Parametric PSF Models

Parametric PSF models represent the point-spread function with analytic formulas. Each model implements evaluate and evaluate_fg (value and gradient), making them efficient for Levenberg-Marquardt fitting.

Models suffixed with "PSF" (e.g. GaussianPSF) sample the continuous PSF. Models suffixed with "PRF" (pixel response function, e.g. GaussianPRF) integrate the PSF over each pixel, modeling the detector pixel response, which can be important for undersampled imaging.

Gaussian Models

CrowdPhot.PSF.CircularGaussianPSF — Type
CircularGaussianPSF(y, x, fwhm, flux, bkg) → CircularGaussianPSF{T}

Circular, symmetric Gaussian PSF with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$ and FWHM given by fwhm. The flux is the integral of the PSF over all space, and bkg is a scalar background level added to the PSF. The model is evaluated by sampling the 2D circularly-symmetric Gaussian function at the given position and adding the background:

\[I(y, x) = \frac{F}{\pi\,\mathrm{FWHM}^2/(4\ln 2)} \exp\!\left[-4\ln 2\, \frac{(x-x_0)^2 + (y-y_0)^2}{\mathrm{FWHM}^2}\right] + B\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, and $B$ is the background level.

Examples

julia> using CrowdPhot.PSF: CircularGaussianPSF

julia> CircularGaussianPSF(2.0, 1.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0) isa CircularGaussianPSF{Float64}
true

CrowdPhot.PSF.GaussianPSF — Type
GaussianPSF(y, x, y_fwhm, x_fwhm, theta, flux, bkg) -> GaussianPSF{T}

General asymmetric Gaussian PSF with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$, FWHM values $\mathrm{FWHM}_y$ and $\mathrm{FWHM}_x$ given by y_fwhm and x_fwhm, and rotated by theta degrees counter-clockwise from the x-axis. The flux is the integral of the PSF over all space, and bkg is a scalar background level added to the PSF.

The model is evaluated by sampling the general 2D Gaussian function at the given position and adding the background:

\[I(y, x) = \frac{F}{\pi\,\mathrm{FWHM}_x\,\mathrm{FWHM}_y/(4\ln 2)} \, \exp\!\left[-4\ln 2\, \left( \frac{u^2}{\mathrm{FWHM}_x^2} + \frac{v^2}{\mathrm{FWHM}_y^2} \right)\right] + B,\]

where

\[u = \cos\theta\,(x-x_0) + \sin\theta\,(y-y_0), \qquad v = -\sin\theta\,(x-x_0) + \cos\theta\,(y-y_0),\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, and $B$ is the background level.

Examples

julia> using CrowdPhot.PSF: GaussianPSF

julia> GaussianPSF(2.0, 1.0, 3.0, 4.0, 35.0, 4.0, 5.0) isa GaussianPSF{Float64}
true

CrowdPhot.PSF.CircularGaussianPRF — Type
CircularGaussianPRF(y, x, fwhm, flux, bkg) -> CircularGaussianPRF{T}

Circular, symmetric Gaussian pixel response function (PRF) with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$ and FWHM given by fwhm. The PRF is the underlying Gaussian PSF integrated analytically over each pixel. The flux is the total flux (sum of PRF values over all pixels equals flux), and bkg is a scalar background level added to the PRF.

The PRF value at detector pixel $(y, x)$ (row, column) is the integral of the Gaussian PSF over the pixel area $[y-0.5, y+0.5] \times [x-0.5, x+0.5]$. The model is evaluated as

\[\begin{aligned} I(y, x) ={}& \frac{F}{4} \left[\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(x + 0.5 - x_0)}{\mathrm{FWHM}}\right) -\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(x - 0.5 - x_0)}{\mathrm{FWHM}}\right)\right] \\ &\times \left[\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(y + 0.5 - y_0)}{\mathrm{FWHM}}\right) -\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(y - 0.5 - y_0)}{\mathrm{FWHM}}\right)\right] + B \end{aligned}\]

where $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, $B$ is the background level, and $\mathrm{erf}$ is the error function.

Examples

julia> using CrowdPhot.PSF: CircularGaussianPRF

julia> CircularGaussianPRF(2.0, 1.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0) isa CircularGaussianPRF{Float64}
true

CrowdPhot.PSF.GaussianPRF — Type
GaussianPRF(y, x, x_fwhm, y_fwhm, theta, flux, bkg) -> GaussianPRF{T}

Asymmetric Gaussian pixel response function (PRF) with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$, FWHM values $\mathrm{FWHM}_x$ and $\mathrm{FWHM}_y$ given by x_fwhm and y_fwhm, and rotated by theta degrees counter-clockwise from the x-axis. The PRF is the underlying Gaussian PSF integrated analytically over each pixel. The flux is the total flux (sum of PRF values over all pixels equals flux), and bkg is a scalar background level added to the PRF.

The PRF value at pixel center $(y, x)$ is the integral of the Gaussian PSF over the pixel area $[y-0.5, y+0.5] \times [x-0.5, x+0.5]$, evaluated after rotating the coordinates by -theta to align with the principal axes. The model is evaluated as

\[\begin{aligned} I(y, x) ={}& \frac{F}{4} \left[\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(u + 0.5)}{\mathrm{FWHM}_x}\right) -\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(u - 0.5)}{\mathrm{FWHM}_x}\right)\right] \\ &\times \left[\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(v + 0.5)}{\mathrm{FWHM}_y}\right) -\mathrm{erf}\!\left(\frac{2\sqrt{\ln 2}\,(v - 0.5)}{\mathrm{FWHM}_y}\right)\right] + B \end{aligned}\]

where

\[\begin{aligned} u &= \cos\theta\,(x - x_0) + \sin\theta\,(y - y_0), \\ v &= -\sin\theta\,(x - x_0) + \cos\theta\,(y - y_0) \end{aligned}\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, and $B$ is the background level.

Moffat Models

Moffat profiles have heavier wings than Gaussians, often providing a better match to ground-based imaging.

CrowdPhot.PSF.CircularMoffatPSF — Type
CircularMoffatPSF(y, x, α, β, flux, bkg) -> CircularMoffatPSF{T}

Circular Moffat PSF with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$, scale length α, wing parameter β, total flux, and background bkg.

The model is evaluated by sampling the Moffat PSF at the given position and adding the background:

\[I(y, x) = F\,\frac{\beta - 1}{\pi\,\alpha^2} \left( 1 + \frac{(x-x_0)^2 + (y-y_0)^2}{\alpha^2} \right)^{-\beta} + B.\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, and $B$ is the background level.

Examples

julia> using CrowdPhot.PSF: CircularMoffatPSF

julia> CircularMoffatPSF(2.0, 1.0, 3.0, 2.5, 4.0, 5.0) isa CircularMoffatPSF{Float64}
true

CrowdPhot.PSF.MoffatPSF — Type
MoffatPSF(y, x, x_α, y_α, theta, β, flux, bkg) -> MoffatPSF{T}

General asymmetric Moffat PSF with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$, scale lengths $\alpha_x$ and $\alpha_y$ given by x_α and y_α, rotation angle theta in degrees counter-clockwise from the x-axis, wing parameter β, total flux, and background bkg.

The model is evaluated by sampling the Moffat PSF at the given position and adding the background:

\[I(y, x) = F\,\frac{\beta - 1}{\pi\,\alpha_x\,\alpha_y} \left( 1 + \frac{u^2}{\alpha_x^2} + \frac{v^2}{\alpha_y^2} \right)^{-\beta} + B,\]

where

\[u = \cos\theta\,(x-x_0) + \sin\theta\,(y-y_0), \qquad v = -\sin\theta\,(x-x_0) + \cos\theta\,(y-y_0).\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, and $B$ is the background level.

Airy Model

CrowdPhot.PSF.AiryPSF — Type
AiryPSF(y, x, radius, flux, bkg) -> AiryPSF{T}

Airy disk point spread function (PSF) with centroid $(y_0, x_0)$, first-dark-ring radius, total flux, and scalar background bkg.

The radius parameter is the radial distance from the centroid to the first dark ring (also called the first zero) of the Airy pattern. This is the limiting angular resolution of an optical system with $R = R_z \lambda/D$, where $D$ is the aperture diameter, $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light, and $R_z \approx 1.2197$ is defined by $J_1(\pi R_z) = 0$.

The model is evaluated by sampling the Airy disk PSF at the given position and adding the background:

\[I(y, x) = \frac{\pi\,F}{4\,a^2} \left(\frac{2\,J_1(u)}{u}\right)^2 + B\]

where

\[r = \sqrt{(x - x_0)^2 + (y - y_0)^2}, \qquad u = \frac{\pi\,r}{a}, \qquad a = \frac{\mathrm{radius}}{R_z}, \qquad R_z = \frac{j_{1,1}}{\pi} \approx 1.2197\]

Here $(y_0, x_0)$ is the centroid, $F$ is the total flux, $B$ is the background level, and $j_{1,1}$ is the first positive zero of the first-order Bessel function $J_1$. The singularity at $u = 0$ is handled analytically; the peak value at the centroid is $\pi\,F/(4a^2) + B$. The FWHM is approximately $0.8437 \times \mathrm{radius}$ along each axis.

julia> AiryPSF(2.0, 1.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0) isa AiryPSF{Float64}

API Internals

CrowdPhot.PSF.GAUSS_PRE — Constant
GAUSS_PRE

Gaussian exponent coefficient for profiles parameterized by FWHM.

\[\mathrm{GAUSS\_PRE} = -4\ln 2\]

For effective radius $r$, the Gaussian exponent is written as

\[\exp\!\left(-4\ln 2\,\frac{r^2}{\mathrm{FWHM}^2}\right) = \exp\!\left(\mathrm{GAUSS\_PRE}\,\frac{r^2}{\mathrm{FWHM}^2}\right).\]

source
CrowdPhot.PSF.AIRY_RZ — Constant
AIRY_RZ

Dimensionless radius of the first dark ring in the Airy pattern.

\[J_1(\pi R_z) = 0, \qquad R_z = \frac{j_{1,1}}{\pi} \approx 1.2197\]

Here $J_1$ is the first-order Bessel function of the first kind, and $j_{1,1}$ is its first positive zero. AIRY_RZ converts the Airy first-dark-ring radius to the effective scale $a = \mathrm{radius}/R_z$ used by AiryPSF.
